Monokai, by Wimer Hazenberg (monokai.nl), 2006: the classic palette of the
original TextMate theme. Its author published no licence for it; the
colours are used with credit to him.

Monokai published no terminal colours; the 16 used here are those of the
Monokai theme built into Visual Studio Code, under the MIT License below.
https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/tree/main/extensions/theme-monokai
